Moving from the streets to the state's coffers, the Ministry of Economy has released the 2024 income tax data, revealing a paradoxical picture of the country's wealth. While total declared incomes grew by four percent, over 11 million Italians—roughly a quarter of all taxpayers—paid absolutely zero income tax due to exemption thresholds and deductions. As *Il Sole 24 Ore* highlights, almost 85 percent of the nation's entire income tax burden is shouldered exclusively by salaried employees and pensioners. The data lays bare a structural imbalance in the Italian machine: a shrinking pool of traditional workers is single-handedly financing the national welfare state, while a massive underground economy and deep regional wealth divides remain practically untouched.
