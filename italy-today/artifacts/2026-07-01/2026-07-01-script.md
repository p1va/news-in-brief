The structural takeaway here goes far beyond retirement planning. For decades, Italian companies relied on holding their employees' severance pay as a form of cheap, internal liquidity. By systematically funneling these funds into financial markets by default, the government is executing a massive transfer of capital from corporate balance sheets to institutional investors, completely reshaping the mechanics of Italian wealth.
