With Italy being the largest beneficiary of the EU's post-pandemic recovery fund, Brussels is watching closely, and this 40-million-euro black hole is exactly the kind of systemic grift the European Commission has been terrified of since the fund's inception.

It is a structural failure of Italy's notoriously leaky aqueduct infrastructure colliding with extreme climate shifts, proving that the Mediterranean summer economy is becoming increasingly difficult to sustain.
