The takeaway here is that Palazzo Chigi has run out of fiscal runway. The next few weeks of budget negotiations will no longer be about what the government can give away, but rather which of its own voters it is willing to disappoint.

The government expects to recover hundreds of millions of euros, signaling a structural shift from relying on manual audits to deploying automated, algorithmic enforcement.

It is a modern, tangible tribute to a saint famous for breaking down barriers.
