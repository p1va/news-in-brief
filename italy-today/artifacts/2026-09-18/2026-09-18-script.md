Between escorting cargo ships in hostile waters and absorbing drone strikes in Saudi Arabia, the government is leaning into a hard-power stance, preparing the public for the financial and physical risks that come with it.

Moving from the frontlines to the home front, a quiet but highly consequential change is coming to the Italian healthcare system. Starting this Monday, September 21st, a new national tariff plan for the National Health Service will officially take effect. This sweeping reform updates the cost of specialized visits, outpatient care, and medical exams. While it introduces public coverage for modern procedures that were previously excluded, it also adjusts the out-of-pocket ticket prices for standard care. This is a classic sleeper story that hasn't dominated the screaming headlines, but it fundamentally alters the daily arithmetic for millions of families trying to access public healthcare amid a cost-of-living crisis.
