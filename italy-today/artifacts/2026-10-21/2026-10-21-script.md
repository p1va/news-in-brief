This quiet, bureaucratic adjustment arrives precisely as ISTAT releases its latest birth rate data, showing a drop of another 14,000 births in 2025, pushing the country further into a demographic winter. With welfare spending now consuming half of the national budget, the structural reality is undeniable: there are simply not enough young workers left to sustain the current system.
